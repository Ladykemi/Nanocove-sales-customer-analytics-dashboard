# NanoCove Sales & Customer Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview
This Power BI project analyzes sales performance, customer behavior, product trends, and revenue distribution for NanoCove, a multinational consumer electronics company.

The goal of the project was to transform raw transactional data into actionable business insights that support strategic decision-making.

---

# Business Problem
NanoCove experienced uneven performance across products, customer segments, and sales channels despite strong overall revenue.

Key business challenges included:

- Underperforming product categories
- Uneven profitability across channels
- Lack of visibility into customer behavior
- Revenue concentration around a few products
- Difficulty identifying high-value customer segments

The business required a centralized analytics solution to improve decision-making across sales, marketing, and operations.

---

# Project Objectives
The dashboard was designed to:

- Analyze product performance
- Track revenue and profitability trends
- Identify top-performing customers
- Understand customer purchasing behavior
- Evaluate store and channel performance
- Support data-driven business strategy

# Business Insights
Some insights discovered from the analysis include:

- A small number of products generated a significant percentage of total revenue.
- Revenue performance varied significantly across store locations.
- Customer purchasing behavior differed by age group and payment method.
- Certain products consistently underperformed and may require pricing or marketing adjustments.
